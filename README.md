# Job Impossible: Gamified Problem Solving in Impossible Spaces

**Job Impossible** is a Unity-based 3D puzzle game that explores unconventional spatial mechanics to create challenging and immersive environments. The project investigates how spatial puzzles can encourage skills relevant to recruitment and employee training, including adaptability, creative problem-solving, and critical thinking.

##About the Project

Traditional recruitment and employee training methods can be time-consuming and resource-intensive. Job Impossible explores an alternative approach through interactive puzzles that challenge players to think differently, adapt to unfamiliar situations, and approach problems from multiple perspectives.

Players navigate seemingly impossible environments, experiment with spatial mechanics, and solve puzzles that challenge their expectations of how space behaves.

## Key Features

- **Recursive Rooms:** Explore spaces that repeat or connect in unexpected ways.
- **Impossible Portals:** Navigate portals that create unconventional spatial connections.
- **Forced Perspective:** Manipulate object sizes by changing their viewing perspective.
- **Lenticular Holograms:** Discover hidden clues visible from specific viewing angles.
- **Perception Portals:** Challenge spatial perception through unconventional environmental connections.
- **Skill-Based Puzzles:** Explore adaptability, creative problem-solving, and critical thinking through gameplay.

*Note: Features are under development*

## Game Rooms

### Room 1 — Recursive Spaces

- **Core Mechanics:** Recursive rooms and impossible portals.
- **Skill Explored:** Adaptability.
- **Objective:** Navigate unexpected spatial connections and adapt to unfamiliar spatial rules.

### Room 2 — Forced Perspective

- **Core Mechanic:** Perspective-based object scaling.
- **Skill Explored:** Creative problem-solving.
- **Objective:** Manipulate objects through changes in perspective to overcome obstacles.

### Room 3 — Perception

- **Core Mechanics:** Lenticular holograms and perception portals.
- **Skills Explored:** Critical thinking and perspective-taking.
- **Objective:** Discover hidden clues and solve puzzles by exploring different viewing angles and challenging initial assumptions.

##Technologies

- **Game Engine:** Unity
- **Programming Language:** C#
- **Graphics and Shaders:** Unity Shader Graph and custom shaders

##Project Objectives

- Develop a playable 3D puzzle game featuring unconventional spatial mechanics.
- Design puzzles that encourage adaptability and creative problem-solving.
- Explore how perspective-based challenges can encourage critical thinking.
- Investigate the potential of game-based challenges for recruitment and employee training.
- Evaluate gameplay through player testing and feedback.
